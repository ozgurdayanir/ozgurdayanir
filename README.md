# Hi 👋, I'm Özgür

### Product Designer & Design Engineer focused on complex products, scalable systems, and thoughtful interfaces.

I work at the intersection of **product design, UX/UI, design systems, and frontend engineering**.

My approach starts with understanding how a product should work — its users, business logic, constraints, edge cases, and technical requirements — before focusing on visual execution.

I've designed and shipped complex digital products including **crypto exchange experiences, trading interfaces, P2P products, staking, conversion flows, hedging tools, SaaS dashboards, and admin systems**.

I also build and maintain **design systems from first principles**, working with Figma Variables, primitive and semantic tokens, reusable components, responsive behavior, and developer handoff.
